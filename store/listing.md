# Google Play listing — Qoriqchi

Matnlar Play Console'ga to'g'ridan-to'g'ri ko'chiriladi. Cheklovlar: nom 30 belgi,
qisqa tavsif 80 belgi, to'liq tavsif 4000 belgi. Har bir variantning uzunligi
qavs ichida ko'rsatilgan.

## Til strategiyasi (muhim)

Play Console'da **o'zbek tili listing tili sifatida yo'q** (2026-yil oktyabrda
tekshirildi: qozoq va qirg'iz bor, o'zbek yo'q). Telefoni o'zbek tilida bo'lgan
foydalanuvchi **standart (default) listingni** ko'radi. Shuning uchun:

1. **Default til — English (United States).** Nomida ham, tavsifida ham o'zbekcha
   kalit so'zlar bo'ladi. Qidiruv shu matnni indekslaydi, shuning uchun o'zbekcha
   so'rovlar ham unga tushadi.
2. **To'liq tavsif ikki tilli:** avval o'zbekcha, keyin inglizcha.
3. **Russian (ru-RU) listing** alohida: O'zbekistonda telefoni rus tilida
   ishlaydiganlar ko'p. Uning tavsifida ham o'zbekcha qism bor.

"O'zbekcha so'rovlarda raqobat yo'q" degani hozircha **faraz**. Reliz qilgandan keyin
Play Console → *Grow users → Store performance → Search terms* bo'limida
qaysi so'zlar haqiqatan o'rnatish olib kelayotganini ko'rib, kalit so'zlarni
o'zgartiramiz.

Qoriqchi Android Accessibility xizmatidan faqat bitta maqsadda foydalanadi: ekranga chiqqan ilovaning paket nomini sizning bloklash ro‘yxatingiz bilan solishtirish. Ekran matni, bosishlar, parollar yoki xabarlar o‘qilmaydi. Bu ma’lumot telefondan tashqariga yuborilmaydi.

Qoriqchi blocks the apps that pull you away during lessons, homework and exam preparation. Open a blocked app and Qoriqchi appears instead — with five English words to recall (B1–B2, for IELTS and CEFR) or the next page of an Uzbek classic.

• Exam countdown on the block screen: "DTM in 87 days".
• One-tap study schedules and a Quick Settings tile.
• Strict sessions with no Stop button (Pro, one-time purchase).
• Honest accounting: Android always lets you turn Accessibility off and Qoriqchi never blocks that, but a session where protection was turned off is recorded as broken and resets your streak.
• Weekly, monthly and yearly statistics; share your week to Telegram as a picture.
• No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. Your lists, sessions and progress stay on your phone; the network is used only by Google Play for the Pro purchase.

Accessibility use: Qoriqchi uses the Accessibility service only to read the package name of the app on screen and compare it with your block list. It does not read screen content, taps, passwords or messages, and none of this leaves the device.

**O'zbekcha:** ilova bloklash, ilovalarni bloklash, telefon qaramligi, telefondan
qutulish, fokus, diqqat, dars, uy vazifasi, imtihon, DTM, attestatsiya, IELTS,
CEFR, inglizcha so'zlar, kitob o'qish, O'tkan kunlar, Instagram bloklash, TikTok
bloklash, Telegram bloklash, vaqtni boshqarish.

**Ruscha:** блокировка приложений, блокировщик, фокус, концентрация, зависимость
от телефона, учёба, экзамен, IELTS.

Kalit so'zlarni ketma-ket ro'yxat qilib yozmang: Google buni spam deb hisoblaydi
(*keyword stuffing*). Ular yuqoridagi matnda gap ichida keladi.
